Negative impact of wearable technology

In today's market of technology watches have increased dramatically for its design and features.Due to the changes in tech on watches it has reduced demand for traditional watches.

Traditional watches have suffered as a result of technology, particularly the emergence of smartwatches, which have caused consumers to become less interested in mechanical timepieces.

Younger generations find smartwatches more appealing due to their multifunctionality, while classic timepieces are experiencing a decline in demand.

In addition to endangering craftsmanship preservation, this change lowers the perceived worth of vintage timepieces.

Moreover, smartwatches have shorter product lifespans due to the quick advancement of technology, in contrast to the timeless appeal and durability of conventional timepieces.
